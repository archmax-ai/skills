# What this wiki is

Meridian Studio is a design studio of about 35 people, working in project teams of three to
six. This wiki holds the knowledge that outlasts any one project: the rules we are held to and
the procedures we repeat.

Two kinds of document live here, and the difference matters when you write one.

* A **policy** says what is allowed and who decides. It changes rarely, by a named owner, and
  is reviewed on a date. Operations owns all of them.
* A **playbook** says what to do, in order, when a recurring situation arises. Whoever runs
  the situation owns the playbook and edits it after each run.

# Where to start
