# Deprecated

**These rules are retired.** Current travel is covered by [travel](travel.md) and reimbursed
under [expenses](expenses.md).

They are kept because projects that started before June 2026 are still billed under the terms
agreed at the time, and a claim filed against one of them is checked against this page.

# The 2024 rules, for reference

Travel was reimbursed as a flat per-diem rather than against receipts: 30 EUR per day within
Germany, 55 EUR elsewhere, with accommodation booked separately by Operations. The per-diem was
dropped in 2026 because it was simultaneously generous for short trips and inadequate for long
ones, and because clients asked for itemised travel costs.
