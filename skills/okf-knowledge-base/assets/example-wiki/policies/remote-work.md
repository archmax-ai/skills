# The rule

Tuesday and Wednesday are studio days: everyone in the same room, because that is when
projects are reviewed together. The rest of the week is yours to place.

# Working elsewhere

Working from another country needs Operations' agreement in advance, and is capped at 20
working days a year — the limit is tax and insurance, not trust. Tell your team before you
change time zone, not after: a project team of four cannot absorb a silent six-hour shift.

New joiners work their first two weeks in the studio, which
[onboarding](../playbooks/new-hire-onboarding.md) assumes.
