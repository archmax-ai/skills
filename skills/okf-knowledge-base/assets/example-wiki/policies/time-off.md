# Allowance

30 days a year, plus public holidays where you are resident. The studio closes between
Christmas and New Year; those days come out of the 30.

# Notice

A day or two: tell your team. A week or more: agree it with your team lead far enough ahead
that the project schedule can absorb it — for a project in delivery, that usually means a
month. Sickness needs no notice, only a message on the day.

All absence goes in the shared calendar, which is what scheduling reads. Nobody tracks who
takes what beyond that.
