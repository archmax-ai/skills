# Approval

All travel is approved before booking: by your team lead within Germany, by Operations for
anything else. Travel a client will pay for is agreed with the client in writing first — until
that exists, it is not billable travel, whatever was said in a meeting.

# Booking and cost

Book through the studio account, not personally, so the studio carries the cancellation risk.
Costs are then reimbursed under [expenses](expenses.md); there is no separate per-diem, which
is the change from [the 2024 rules](travel-2024.md).

Billable travel is passed through to the client at cost and appears as its own line on the
invoice, per [invoice a client](../playbooks/invoice-a-client.md).
