The studio reimburses what you spend to do your work. Anything a reasonable colleague would
question, ask about before you spend it rather than after.

# Limits

| Category | Limit | Approval |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Meals while travelling | 45 EUR per day | none below the limit |
| Taxi or ride-hailing | 40 EUR per trip | team lead above the limit |
| Software licence | 200 EUR per year | Operations, always |
| Equipment | any | Operations, always |

Limits are per person per day and come from the 2026 finance handbook.[^finance-handbook]

# How to claim

File within 30 days of the spend, with a receipt showing the amount and the date. Claims for a
client project need the project code, since billable items are passed through at cost — see
[invoice a client](../playbooks/invoice-a-client.md).

Travel is claimed the same way, but needs approval *before* it is booked: see
[travel](travel.md).

[^finance-handbook]: Finance handbook, 2026 edition
