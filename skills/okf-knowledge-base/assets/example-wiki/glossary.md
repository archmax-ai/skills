Several of these words mean something narrower here than they do in the trade. Where a term
has a policy or playbook behind it, that document is the authority and this entry is only a
pointer.

| Term | Meaning here |
|------|--------------|
| **Studio day** | A working day booked to a client project rather than to internal work. The unit we quote, schedule and invoice in. |
| **Kickoff** | The meeting that opens a project and fixes its scope, named contacts and billing terms. See [client kickoff](playbooks/client-kickoff.md). |
| **Handover** | The package a client receives at the end of a project: source files, licences and a written summary. |
| **Billable travel** | Travel a client has agreed to pay for, agreed in writing before it is booked. See [travel](policies/travel.md). |
| **Retainer** | A fixed number of studio days reserved per month, invoiced whether or not they are used. |
