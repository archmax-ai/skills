# Trigger

A signed statement of work. The project lead runs the kickoff within a week of signature.

# In the meeting

1. Name the client's decision-maker — the one person who can approve work. A project without
   one runs twice.
2. Agree the scope in studio days, and what is explicitly out of it.
3. Agree billing: the invoicing rhythm, the purchase-order number if the client needs one, and
   whether travel is billable under [travel](../policies/travel.md).
4. Agree what the handover contains, so [offboarding](client-offboarding.md) is not a
   negotiation.

# Afterwards

Send a written summary the same day and ask for a reply confirming it. That reply is what
[invoice a client](invoice-a-client.md) relies on when an invoice is queried.
