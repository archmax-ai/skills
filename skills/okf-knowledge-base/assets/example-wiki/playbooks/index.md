# Playbooks

What to do, in order, when a recurring situation arises. Each is owned by whoever runs the
situation and is edited after a run that went badly.

* [New-hire onboarding](new-hire-onboarding.md) - The first two weeks of a new joiner, and who owns each part of them.
* [Client kickoff](client-kickoff.md) - Opening a project: scope, contacts and billing terms, fixed in writing.
* [Client offboarding](client-offboarding.md) - Closing a project and handing over the files a client is owed.
* [Invoice a client](invoice-a-client.md) - Turning booked studio days and pass-through costs into an invoice.
* [Security incident](security-incident.md) - First hour after a suspected breach of studio or client data.
