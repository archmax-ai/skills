# Trigger

A signed contract with a start date. Operations runs this playbook; the team lead owns days
three onward.

# Before day one

1. Laptop ordered and configured, accounts created, calendar invitations sent for the first
   week.
2. A buddy named — someone outside the new joiner's project team.

# Day one and two

Both days are in the studio, which [remote work](../policies/remote-work.md) requires. Walk
through the [company handbook](../company-handbook.md) together rather than sending a link,
then the three policies that apply immediately:
[remote work](../policies/remote-work.md), [time off](../policies/time-off.md) and
[expenses](../policies/expenses.md).

# First two weeks

The new joiner shadows one project through a [kickoff](client-kickoff.md) if the calendar
allows it. At the end of week two, the team lead asks what in this wiki was wrong or missing
and fixes it — that is the only review of these pages that reliably happens.
