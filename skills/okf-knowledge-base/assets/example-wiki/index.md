# Meridian Studio wiki

The operating knowledge of a 35-person design studio: the rules people are held to and the
procedures they follow. Start with the handbook.

# Concepts

* [Company handbook](company-handbook.md) - How the studio runs day to day, and which policy or playbook answers which question.
* [Glossary](glossary.md) - Terms used across the studio, with the meaning the studio gives them.

# Subdirectories

* [policies](policies/index.md) - Rules people are held to, owned by Operations.
* [playbooks](playbooks/index.md) - Step-by-step procedures for recurring situations.
